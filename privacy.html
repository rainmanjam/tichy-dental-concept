Privacy Policy

How Tichy Dental handles information connected to this website.

Effective October 13, 2026 · Last updated October 13, 2026

Information we collect

This website does not collect personal or health information directly, and it does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Appointment requests, patient registration, and consent forms are completed through our secure patient portal, which is operated by our practice-management provider and protected under HIPAA.

If you call or email us, we use what you share only to respond to you and to provide care. Please don't send medical details or other sensitive information by email; use the secure patient portal instead.

Third-party services

Fonts are hosted on this website. The office map loads from Google Maps only when you choose to open it. Links to Google, Yelp, Facebook, Instagram, X, and product manufacturers take you to sites governed by their own privacy policies.

Your health information

Our Notice of Privacy Practices explains how protected health information is used and disclosed under HIPAA. Ask our front desk for a copy, or call (503) 635-3588.
